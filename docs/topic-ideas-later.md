# Checklist topics to consider later

**Status:** ideas only, noted 8 October 2026. Not in the tool. For John Smith (CPS subject-matter expert) and the project owner to consider when the topic list is next reviewed.

These come from general CPS practice rather than the specific sources reviewed for the current definitions, so they need an expert's judgement on whether, and where, they belong.

## 1. Description of the injury itself

- **What:** where on the body the injury is, its size, and its current state, as reported by the parent and the child.
- **Why:** this is currently scattered across the two account topics. A topic of its own would make it clear when it has been asked, and would feed the "injury description as reported" form field directly.
- **Question for John Smith:** should the worker's own observation of the injury count here, or only what the family says?

## 2. Who the child can talk to (trusted adults)

- **What:** people the child says they trust or would tell if something were wrong: a relative, a teacher, a neighbour.
- **Why:** safety assessments usually look at protective factors, not only risks, and this feeds directly into the safety plan (topic 11).
- **Question for John Smith:** does this belong in this interview, or in a later visit?

## 3. Standard safety screening areas

- **What:** areas commonly covered in CPS safety assessments, such as violence between adults in the home, substance use, and caregiver stress.
- **Why:** they are part of many safety assessments.
- **Questions for John Smith:**
  - Do these belong in this interview type, or in a separate use case pack (the tool supports several)?
  - How should the tool handle them, given they are sensitive questions the worker may want to ask in a particular order or setting?

## How to add one

Each topic needs, in the same style as `docs/topic-definitions-draft.md`: required parts, what counts as partial, when follow-up is required or suggested, and one invented example. New topics go into a new version of the pack and are re-tested against the comparison script before use.
